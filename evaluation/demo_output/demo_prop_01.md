Text: Transfer of Property Act, 1882 - Section 106: Duration of certain leases in absence of written contract or local usage
[Chapter V - Of Leases of Immoveable Property]

In the absence of a contract or local law or usage to the contrary, a lease of immoveable property for agricultural or manufacturing purposes shall be deemed to be a lease from year to year, terminable, on the part of either lessor or lessee, by six months' notice; and a lease of immoveable property for any other purpose shall be deemed to be a lease from month to month, terminable, on the part of either lessor or lessee, by fifteen days' notice.
