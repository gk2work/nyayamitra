Text: Indian Contract Act, 1872 - Section 73: Compensation for loss or damage caused by breach of contract
[Chapter VI - Of the Consequences of Breach of Contract]

When a contract has been broken, the party who suffers by such breach is entitled to receive, as compensation for any loss or damage caused to him thereby, such compensation as would in the ordinary course of things have been caused by such breach, and such compensation is not to be given for any remote and indirect loss or damage sustained by reason of the breach.
