[Chapter IV - Jurisdiction and Power of Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission]

A complaint may be filed with the District Commission, the State Commission or the National Commission, as the case may be, by the consumer to whom the goods are sold or delivered or agreed to be sold or delivered, or the service is provided or agreed to be provided; or any recognized consumer association; or one or more consumers, where there are numerous consumers having the same interest, with the permission of the Commission; or the Central Government or the Central Authority; or the State Government.
